01 · The basics

What is an iPhone?

iPhone is Apple's line of smartphones. It combines a touchscreen, cameras, internet access, apps, and communication tools in one device. iPhones run Apple's iOS operating system and can work with other Apple products and services, while also supporting many third-party apps.

📱

The device

A display, processor, cameras, microphones, speakers, sensors, and rechargeable battery. Size and hardware vary between models.

🧩

Apps

Built-in apps support everyday tasks. Additional apps are available through Apple's App Store; availability may vary by location.

☁️

Apple services

Services such as iCloud can sync selected information across devices. An iPhone can also be used on its own.

02 · What it can do

Popular features

Features depend on the model, iOS version, country, and carrier. Always check the specifications of a specific iPhone for exact details.

📷

Camera & video

Capture photos and video, make video calls, and edit media. Camera lenses and advanced modes differ by model.

💬

Calls & messages

Call people and send messages. Cellular service requires a compatible carrier plan; Wi-Fi supports internet use and some calling options.

🔐

Security

Protect your device with a passcode and, on supported models, Face ID or Touch ID. Software updates can include important security fixes.

🗺️

Maps & location

Use maps for directions and nearby places. Control which apps can access your location in Privacy settings.

♿

Accessibility

Options can include VoiceOver, captions, text-size controls, display adjustments, and other tools to suit different needs.

🔋

Battery & charging

Battery life changes with usage, settings, network, and battery age. Charging ports and wireless charging support vary by model.

03 · Software

Getting started with iOS

iOS is the operating system that powers iPhone. It includes the Home Screen, apps, notifications, privacy controls, and Settings. Apple releases updates over time, and supported versions depend on the iPhone model.

Make it yours

Arrange apps and widgets, choose a wallpaper, and adjust display, sound, and notification settings.

Back up your information

Set up iCloud or computer backups so you can restore data when replacing or resetting a phone. Review what is included.

Review privacy

Check app access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, and photos. Allow only the access you are comfortable with.

Helpful note: Menus and features can change with iOS updates. For current instructions, consult Apple Support or the Tips app on your iPhone.

04 · A short timeline

iPhone over the years

A few milestones show how the product has changed. This is a brief overview, not a complete list of models.

2007
Apple introduced the first iPhone, combining a phone, music player, and internet features with a multi-touch display.
2008
The App Store launched, letting people download third-party apps to iPhone.
2010s
New generations brought faster hardware, larger display choices, improved cameras, and features including Touch ID and Face ID.
Today
Different models offer different sizes and capabilities. Lineups, specifications, and availability change over time.
05 · Before you buy

Choosing an iPhone

Start with your needs and budget. Compare current specifications and local prices rather than relying on a model name alone.

Size & display

Consider screen size, one-handed use, and how the phone feels to carry. Try one in person if practical.

Camera

Compare camera hardware and modes based on how you take photos and videos. Not every model has the same system.

Storage

Allow space for apps, photos, videos, and downloads. Cloud storage can help but does not replace all on-device storage.

Price & condition

Compare new, refurbished, and used devices. For a used phone, check its condition, battery, activation status, and return terms.

Carrier compatibility

Confirm network support, SIM or eSIM requirements, and carrier compatibility in your area before purchase.

Updates & repairs

Check software compatibility and repair options. Supported features and updates vary by model.

06 · Everyday care

Tips for using your iPhone

Keep software current

Install available iOS updates and protect the phone with a strong passcode.

Back up regularly

Check that backups are working and keep your Apple Account recovery details up to date.

Choose trusted accessories

Use compatible charging accessories from reputable sources and follow Apple's care guidance.

Look after the battery

Review Battery settings and avoid exposing the phone to extreme temperatures.
